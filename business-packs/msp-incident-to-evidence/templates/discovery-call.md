# Customer Discovery Call — Operational Resilience Baseline

**Goal:** discover whether a real customer problem exists. This is not a scripted pitch, a risk assessment, or authorization to access customer systems.

## Before the call

- Identify why this organization might care about operational continuity.
- Prepare no more than 5–7 open questions.
- Do not ask for credentials, confidential incident details, or raw personal data.
- Be clear that the current starter service is manual-first and narrowly scoped.

## Opening

“I'm developing a practical service to help smaller IT teams document one operational risk or incident, identify what is known versus still unproven, and leave with a prioritized action plan. I'm trying to understand how teams handle this today. Could I ask a few questions about your process? This is discovery, not a request for system access.”

## Questions

1. Think about the last significant outage, failed change, or difficult recovery. What made it hard to understand or resolve?
2. How do you currently connect service-desk tickets, infrastructure state, approvals, and evidence that a fix worked?
3. When a ticket is closed, what tells you the underlying issue is actually resolved?
4. How do you know a backup or recovery procedure can restore the service you care about?
5. What part of documenting, reviewing, or following up on these issues consumes the most time?
6. What tools or providers are already central to this workflow? What would be painful to replace?
7. Have you paid for help with this problem before? What did you buy, and what was missing?
8. Who decides whether a small fixed-scope engagement is worth paying for?
9. What would a useful deliverable need to contain for you to act on it?
10. What would make this service unacceptable or too risky?

## Offer only after understanding the problem

Describe the Baseline Sprint scope and exclusions. Do not imply that Luminous has a working live PSA connector, autonomous remediation, ransomware immunity, or a compliance certification. If their problem requires production actuation, forensic work, or regulated-sector assurance that you cannot competently provide, say so and refer them to an appropriately qualified provider.

## Discovery outcome

- [ ] Qualified problem and accountable buyer
- [ ] Problem exists, but no buying signal yet
- [ ] Wrong customer or low priority
- [ ] Requires capability/qualification not currently available
- [ ] Follow-up agreed

The evidence from this call is about demand and customer needs, not proof that the platform is effective.
