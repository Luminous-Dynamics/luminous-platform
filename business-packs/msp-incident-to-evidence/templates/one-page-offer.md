# Operational Resilience Baseline

**A focused review that turns one operational concern into a clear, source-linked action plan.**

## Who it is for

Smaller businesses and IT service providers that need a clearer view of one service, incident, change process, or recovery gap without replacing their existing service desk or infrastructure tools.

## What you receive

- A documented scope and environment boundary.
- Review of customer-approved records and evidence for one selected service or operational concern.
- Clear separation of observed facts, customer-provided assertions, and unknowns.
- A prioritized action register with owners, dates, and acceptance evidence.
- A handoff meeting and report listing limitations and next steps.

## How it works

1. Agree the scope, acceptance criteria, and data-handling terms.
2. Review only the approved records and environment.
3. Document evidence, gaps, owners, and safe next steps.
4. Review the result with your team.

## What it does not include

This baseline is not a penetration test, forensic investigation, full security audit, regulatory certification, guarantee of recovery, or autonomous remediation service. Production changes and destructive tests require a separately agreed scope and an appropriately qualified method.

## What we need from you

A named decision-maker, a technical contact, the selected service/incident, approved source information, and agreement on data retention and acceptance criteria. No passwords or private keys are required for the baseline.

## Pricing

Quote a fixed scope after a short discovery call. The quotation lists included work, exclusions, timing, fees/taxes where applicable, and how additional work is approved.
