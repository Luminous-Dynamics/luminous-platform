## 4. Explicit exclusions

Unless a separate written scope is approved, this work does not include:
- penetration testing, forensic investigation, or incident response;
- production configuration changes or destructive testing;
- privileged remote actions or unattended remediation;
- legal advice, regulatory certification, or independent assurance;
- handling credentials or secrets in reports or evidence bundles;
- integrations or platform capabilities not explicitly listed as implemented and qualified.

## 5. Customer responsibilities

- Provide accurate source information and a named authorized contact.
- Approve the scope, source data, delivery window, and any access.
- Remove secrets and unnecessary personal data from submitted exports.
- Identify applicable data handling, retention, and incident escalation rules.
- Decide and authorize any remediation outside this engagement.

## 6. Provider responsibilities

- Work only within the agreed scope and approved access.
- Keep the tenant and asset boundary explicit.
- Distinguish observed facts, customer assertions, simulations, and unproven items.
- Protect evidence using the agreed storage and transfer method.
- Escalate unexpected exposure, target ambiguity, or a safety issue; pause when safe execution is not established.
- Deliver limitations and unresolved items with the report.
