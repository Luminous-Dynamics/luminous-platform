# Commercial licensing inquiries

This document is a contact point for commercial licensing discussions. **It is not itself a commercial license or a grant of additional rights.**

Luminous Dynamics publishes components under their respective open-source licenses. Licensing is component-specific: the license of this repository does not automatically apply to every dependency, sibling repository, crate, or integrated deployment.

For components whose rights holders can offer an additional commercial license, organizations whose intended use is incompatible with the applicable open-source license may request a separate discussion. Any additional rights exist only when they are documented in a written agreement signed by the relevant rights holder and the licensee.

A licensing inquiry should identify:

- The exact repository, crate, version, and commit to be covered.
- The intended use (internal deployment, hosted service, distribution, modification, or embedding in a larger product).
- The organizations and jurisdictions involved.
- Whether source modifications or customer redistribution are expected.
- Any support, maintenance, or delivery requirements.

Third-party dependencies remain subject to their own licenses and terms. A commercial agreement for one Luminous Dynamics component does not automatically relicense third-party software or other projects.
